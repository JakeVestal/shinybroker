Looks up the assets at IBKR whose names or symbols approximately match a search
string. The search string can be the name or part of the name of an asset (e.g.,
 "Apple"), or it can be a ticker (e.g., "AAPL"). Results are separated into
 "stocks" and "bonds". "Stocks" includes "stock-like" contracts such as ETFs.
 This tool is useful if you know the name or ticker symbol of an asset you're
 interested in and would like to know its Contract ID, or check the exchanges on
  which it's traded.
